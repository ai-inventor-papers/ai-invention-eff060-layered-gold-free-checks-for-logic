## 11. Power (corrected rationale)

From E's L25 numbers, the stratified ΔAUROC CI half-width was 0.085 at 300 sentences (873 A+B rows, 74 sentences with both classes), so SE ≈ 0.043. SE scales as 1/sqrt(sentences with both classes). Detecting Δ = 0.08 at 80% power needs SE ≤ 0.0286, which is about 690 L25 sentences at E's yield. 350 L25 sentences give MDE80 ≈ 0.112, and 450 give ≈ 0.099. **The direction's claim that ~400 L25 sentences detect +0.08 is wrong.** An L25 null on E2 with |Δ| < 0.10 is 'underpowered', not 'negative'.
